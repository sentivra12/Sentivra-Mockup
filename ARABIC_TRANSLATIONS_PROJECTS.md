## General Translation Notes

### Technical Terms Kept in English:
- **ERP, CRM, MRP** - Widely recognized acronyms in Arabic business context
- **API** - Standard term (if used)

### Common Patterns:
- **System** → نظام
- **Platform** → منصة
- **Dashboard** → لوحة تحكم
- **Management** → إدارة
- **Tracking** → تتبع
- **Integration** → تكامل
- **Real-time** → في الوقت الفعلي / فوري
- **Advanced** → متقدم
- **Comprehensive** → شامل

### Style Guidelines Followed:
1. ✅ **Formal MSA** - Appropriate for business context
2. ✅ **No colloquialisms** - Professional tone maintained
3. ✅ **Consistent terminology** - Same terms for same concepts
4. ✅ **Natural phrasing** - Not word-for-word literal translation
5. ✅ **Clear structure** - Logical flow in Arabic
